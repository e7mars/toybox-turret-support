Easy to start, deep enough to keep

Your thumb moves. Your buddy fires.

You can begin without a rulebook. The choices stack up as you learn the worlds.

  1. ↗

    Move through the mess

    Drag, brake, and Dash out of the marked danger. Popper fires automatically.

  2. ✦

    Pick a boost

    Collect stars and choose one clear upgrade whenever the action pauses.

  3. ◎

    Beat a boss

    Boss wins add a weapon or improve one. A second weapon is your choice.

Make it yours

Ten weapons. Four worlds. Plenty of personality.

Try a direct Bubble Beam, a bouncing Button Boomerang, a Candy Mortar, and more. Build toward five ranks, unlock worlds by beating four bosses, and dress up your buddy with looks earned by playing.

  • Boss moves tell you where danger will land.
  • Each world brings its own toys, terrain, and boss roster.
  • Outfits and keepsakes stay cosmetic—your choices and movement win the run.
