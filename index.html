A little help

Back to the toybox.

Toybox Turret is a single-player game by Oytun Yegin. Dodge crowds, beat bosses, and dress up your buddy.

Contact support

Report a problem or ask a question through the game's GitHub support page.

Open a support issue

Issues are public. Include only details you want to share.

Tell us what happened

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, game version, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help.

Saved progress

Your looks, world unlocks, and personal bests stay on your device. There is no account or cloud save. Deleting the app may remove them.

If the game says progress has not saved, keep it open and use Retry save. If progress could not be loaded, use Retry load.

Sound and motion

Open Settings to change sound effects, music, vibration, or reduced motion. How to play explains the controls and upgrades.
