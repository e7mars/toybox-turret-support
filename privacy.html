Overview

Toybox Survival is a single-player mobile game. This policy explains the information used by the game, its optional public rankings, and its optional rewarded ads. The game does not offer a game account, subscription, paid Star Token pack, checkout, or cloud-save service.

This policy describes the current earned-only game and its launch configuration. TestFlight builds can use Google demo ads instead of revenue-earning ads; advertising may also be unavailable while provider verification is pending. Earlier private builds with only local scores are described in the archived beta policy.

Eligible players can choose a separate, optional gameplay-analysis setting. That feature is described below. It is not enabled by local support reports, public rankings, advertising consent, or a previous build.

Game data on your device

Your game progress, gear, settings, selected age range, local nickname, and interrupted-run recovery are stored on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data can remove that local progress. We do not use this local game save as a cloud-save service.

The game opens directly. When you choose a rewarded ad, public rankings, or gameplay analytics, you can skip the age choice or pick Under 13, 13–15, 16–17, or 18 or older. The game reuses your first saved choice.

A selected range stays on your device, including a range selected in an earlier version of the game. For eligible ad requests, the app sends age-treatment and under-age-of-consent settings to Google rather than a birthday. Public rankings and sharing are available only to profiles set to 16–17 or 18 or older.

Optional public rankings

Public rankings are available only to profiles set to 16–17 or 18 or older. A fresh player sees the local age-range choice before joining rankings. To appear on a ranking board, a player first chooses a public nickname. Please do not use a real name, email address, or other private information in that nickname.

After a nickname is saved, eligible world runs are automatically queued and sent to the ranking service when the game is open and online, including otherwise eligible XP Magnet runs. Endless locks its ranked result at the fourth boss or submits the score when the run ends earlier. Later laps and Daily scores count toward personal records. A monthly-prize candidate needs at least one boss win or at least 30 seconds and 100 Combat score. The service receives the public nickname, a random installation identifier, a run identifier, world, Combat score, elapsed time, boss wins, and the relevant monthly period. The nickname, Combat score, elapsed time, boss wins, and rank can be visible to other players. Combat scores are labelled unverified.

All-star placement compares base Combat score before the 1×/2×/3× challenge multiplier, while each star tab shows that tier’s raw Combat scores. A completed run that crosses a UTC-month boundary can receive up to six hours of submission grace. This does not guarantee delivery while the device is offline. After the grace expires, the saved result remains on the device but cannot enter global placement for that month.

The ranking service and its network providers can also process IP address and request metadata to protect the service and apply rate limits. This network information is not shown on the public board.

The random identifiers are not displayed on the public board. Submitted ranking records can remain on the board until they are removed or the board is retired. Uninstalling the game does not remove a record that has already been posted. To request help with a public ranking record, use the Support page and provide only the public nickname, world, and score needed to find it.

Optional rewarded ads and privacy choices

Profiles set to Under 13 are not offered Google advertising or public rankings. When a fresh player first chooses a rewarded ad, the game first presents the local age-range choice. For profiles set to 13–15, 16–17, or 18 or older, a player may choose to watch an optional Google rewarded ad. No ad is required to play, unlock a world, or finish a run.

A rewarded ad can enable XP Magnet for the current run. After the first boss, an available rewarded ad can also grant one Star Token, subject to the game’s daily limit. A failed, unavailable, cancelled, or incomplete ad grants no reward, and the player can always continue without it.

The app requests non-personalized ads and applies its age-appropriate ad settings. Before it requests a rewarded ad, it updates consent information, presents any required privacy form, and checks whether ads may be requested. If Google requires a privacy-options entry point, the game shows Ad privacy choices in Settings → Game.

Non-personalized ads can still involve information processing by Google and its service providers, such as device or advertising identifiers, IP-derived information, ad interactions, diagnostics, and information used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and service operation. Google describes its handling in its Privacy Policy and its iOS SDK data disclosure.

Sharing

For profiles that have selected an age range permitting public features, Share opens the iOS share sheet only after the player taps it. A player chooses the recipient and can cancel. A shared run card may include the buddy name, outfit, world, score, boss wins, and build. The game does not automatically send a share card to the publisher.

A card or message may also contain the public game website link. Image sharing uses a temporary image on your device and attempts to remove it after the share sheet closes. If you choose Save Image, iOS may ask for permission to save to Photos. Sharing does not select or read your photo library. A saved image or a copy received by someone else can remain after the game is deleted.

Local support reports

The game keeps limited game-quality counts and event labels on your device, including general error categories. Daily counts and video-reward failure categories are limited to 31 UTC days; recent event labels are limited to 200 entries. Video-reward failures contain a day and a general reason, not the raw Google response, ad-unit identifier, or request identifier.

The current game has no publisher diagnostics upload endpoint and does not automatically upload these reports. Eligible profiles can choose Share support report in Settings → Game → About & privacy. The report goes through the system share sheet to a recipient you choose. The export excludes the installation identifier and upload endpoint; it does not contain your buddy name, scores, precise location, advertising identifiers, or raw crash details. Google's separate SDK diagnostics are described in the advertising section above.

Optional gameplay analysis

Profiles set to 16–17 or 18 or older can choose Share run analytics in Settings → Run analytics. A fresh player sees the local age-range choice before this setting can be turned on. The setting is off until the player turns it on. It is a separate choice from public rankings, support reports, Google ad privacy choices, and sharing a run card. An older local diagnostics choice is not carried into this setting. Profiles set to Under 13 or 13–15 are not offered this setting.

With that choice on, the game creates a random analytics identifier and a separate device-held secret for this service. They are not a public nickname and are not the ranking identifier or the private support key. The service stores a hash of the secret rather than the secret itself to accept later requests. It does not provide a public player profile, history, leaderboard, or search route.

The publisher can review this pseudonymous history in private reports to assess game balance and feature use. It is not public and is not joined to your ranking, support, or advertising identifiers.

The game sends a bounded run history: app and balance versions; broad platform and release channel; world, difficulty and challenge context; the starting item loadout, item stat lines and grades, calculated modifiers, and progression summary; periodic run state snapshots; final weapons, ranks, augments, combo routes and combat counters; and whether run assistance was used. It also records the draft choices offered, selected or rerolled, and discrete replay, share-sheet and rewarded-ad actions. A player may optionally rate a finished run as Rough, Okay or Fun. This is fixed-choice feedback, not a free-text survey.

A stat-bearing equipped combat item can use an item identifier that begins with look:; its identity and stat lines are included because they affect the run. The history does not include a purely cosmetic outfit or wardrobe snapshot.

The separate Share gear inventory setting sends saved item ownership, equipped slots, stat levels, and coin and token balances using the same random analytics identifier. Turning it off stops future inventory sharing. Delete shared run records also removes the live inventory history.

The history does not include a nickname, buddy name, age range, email, contacts, precise location, advertising identifier, ad-unit or request identifier, device model, IP address in the stored run record, raw crash data, message content, recipient, share-card image, or a record of whether a share was completed. Network providers can process request and IP information to route and protect the service, as described below.

The service automatically removes analytics records 90 days after it receives them. The on-device queue is limited to 31 days, 300 events and 2 MiB, and is kept only to retry an opted-in upload. Turning the setting off stops new collection. The player can also choose Delete shared run records in Settings → Run analytics: that disables the setting and asks the service to remove the live analytics records. If the device is offline, the deletion request stays on the device until it can be delivered. The service retains the random analytics identifier, a one-way credential hash and deletion timing for 90 days only to reject delayed queued uploads; this marker has no run history. Existing protected backup storage keeps up to seven daily SQLite snapshots, so a removed record can remain until its normal rotation.

Your choices and stored records

You can play without selecting an age range, choosing a public nickname, sharing a card, watching an ad, or turning on gameplay analytics. Where required, use Ad privacy choices in Settings to revisit Google's privacy form. Sound, music, vibration and reduced motion can also be changed in Settings.

Deleting the app can remove local progress and settings. It does not remove an already-submitted ranking record, a support request, or a shared image. It can also prevent delivery of an analytics deletion request that has not yet reached the service. Ranking records, submission receipts and installation-verification records are retained to operate the boards, prevent duplicate submissions, and deliver earned ranking rewards. There is no automatic deletion when a monthly board closes. Protected backup copies may remain after a live record is removed until those backups are replaced.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Contact the publisher using the support route below. We may need enough information to locate the record and verify the request; a public nickname alone is not proof of ownership. You may also contact your local privacy regulator.

Website, support and service providers

Music lab includes one complete soundtrack on the device. If you choose to download another set, GitHub serves the audio files and receives the network information needed to deliver them, including your IP address. The downloaded music can then play offline.

The support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Its page code adds no analytics, tracking pixels, advertising cookies or automatic app launches. GitHub can process visitor IP addresses and request information for security and operation. See GitHub's privacy statement.

GitHub support issues are public: your GitHub profile, message and attachments can be visible to other people. Use them only for non-sensitive questions and bug reports. The publisher uses what you choose to provide to respond and investigate problems.

Public-ranking and opted-in analytics requests pass through Cloudflare to the publisher's service. Cloudflare, GitHub, Google and their service providers may process information in countries other than yours. Their handling is described in their privacy information, including Cloudflare's privacy policy and the Google and GitHub links above.

Private help and privacy messages

You can send a private message through this website's private support form. Updated game builds also offer Help & privacy under Settings → Game → About & privacy → Help. The website and game have separate conversations. No email address or game account is required. If you are under 13, ask a parent or guardian to help contact us.

Only the topic and message you write are sent as message content; scores, names, age ranges and local diagnostic reports are not attached automatically. A separate random conversation key authenticates requests, and its hash is stored by the service. The service retains your messages, developer replies, message receipts and timestamps. Network information is processed for delivery and abuse prevention. Messages are private to the conversation and the publisher's support operator, not posted on public boards or GitHub issues.

Return to the same in-game screen or browser to check for replies; no email or push notification is sent. The private key and any message awaiting confirmation are saved locally. Deleting the app, clearing browser storage or losing the key can remove access to that conversation. Conversations are automatically removed after 90 days without a new message, with cleanup performed periodically. Protected backups can remain until they rotate. You can request earlier removal through the private conversation.

We use these records to respond to your request and protect the service. Do not send passwords, payment details, a child's real name, birthdays, or private device/conversation keys. For a ranking request, start with the public nickname and world. We may ask for proportionate information to verify ownership before changing another record.

Questions and requests

Use private support for privacy questions or help locating a public ranking record. Please do not include passwords, full device identifiers, a child’s real name, birthday, or other sensitive information in a support request.
